Contributing

BON in a Box is a community-driven initiative that brings together scientific methods and expertise to support biodiversity monitoring and reporting.

We welcome contributions from researchers, developers, and organizations interested in making biodiversity analyses more accessible, reproducible, and widely applicable.

By contributing to the Biodiversity Evaluation Engine (BEE), you can help translate scientific research into practical tools that support biodiversity monitoring, conservation planning, and policy reporting.

Why contribute?

Researchers around the world are developing methods to assess biodiversity change, but these methods are often difficult to access or apply beyond their original research context.

Contributing to BON in a Box helps make these methods available to a broader community while supporting open science, reproducibility, and collaboration.

Contributors can:

  • Increase the impact of their research by making analytical methods accessible to researchers, practitioners, and decision-makers.

  • Support reproducible science by sharing transparent, standardized analytical workflows.

  • Collaborate with the GEO BON community to improve and expand existing methods.

  • Support biodiversity monitoring and reporting by helping countries and organizations calculate Essential Biodiversity Variables (EBVs) and biodiversity indicators.

What can you contribute?

We welcome several types of contributions, including:

  • New analytical pipelines: Methods for calculating EBVs, biodiversity indicators, or other metrics relevant to biodiversity monitoring.

  • Improvements to existing pipelines: Updates to scientific methods, additional functionality, new data sources, or improvements to performance and usability.

  • Individual scripts and technical contributions: Reusable components, bug fixes, and improvements to the underlying platform.

  • Documentation and testing: Improvements that make BEE easier to understand, use, and maintain.

Is your analysis pipeline a good fit?

We are particularly interested in pipelines that:

  • Calculate biodiversity metrics relevant to monitoring, conservation planning, and policy reporting.

  • Are open source and developed using supported programming languages, such as R, Python, or Julia.

  • Can be applied across different species, countries, regions, or monitoring contexts.

  • Can be automated and reproduced using clearly defined inputs, analytical steps, and outputs.

Pipelines do not need to be fully developed or meet all technical requirements before you contact us. The BON in a Box team can help assess whether a proposed contribution is suitable and identify what may be needed for integration.

For more details, see our Pipeline Standards.

How to contribute a pipeline

We encourage contributors to get in touch early in the development process so that we can work together to make pipelines reusable, reproducible, and compatible with BEE.

Step 1. Get in touch

Contact the BON in a Box team at boninabox@geobon.org to discuss your proposed contribution.

We can help determine whether your method is a good fit, identify existing components that could be reused, and discuss the resources and technical support needed for integration.

Step 2. Design your pipeline

Develop a high-level overview of the analytical workflow, including its inputs, processing steps, and expected outputs.

The BON in a Box team can help identify opportunities to make the pipeline modular, generalizable, and compatible with existing BEE components.

We recommend starting with a simple, functional version before adding more complex features.

Step 3. Develop and test

Develop your pipeline following the Pipeline Standards, which outline requirements for documentation, reproducibility, usability, and technical integration.

Contributors can work with the BON in a Box team to test their pipelines and ensure they function correctly within BEE.

Step 4. Peer review

Once a pipeline is complete and meets the required standards, it can be submitted for peer review.

The review process evaluates the scientific methodology, reproducibility, documentation, and usability of the pipeline.

Contributors may be asked to make revisions based on reviewer feedback before the pipeline is accepted.

For more information, see our Peer Review Process.

Step 5. Publication and continued development

Once accepted, the pipeline can be published through BON in a Box and made available for use by the wider community.

Published pipelines can receive a DOI, allowing the contribution to be identified and cited.

Contributors are encouraged to continue improving their pipelines as new methods, datasets, and scientific knowledge become available.

Other ways to contribute

You do not need to develop a complete analytical pipeline to contribute to BON in a Box.

We also welcome contributions to existing pipelines, documentation, testing, and software development.

You can explore our open-source repositories on the GEO BON GitHub organization or contact the team to discuss opportunities to contribute.

Interested in contributing?

Whether you have an existing analytical method, an idea for a new pipeline, or would like to support the development of BEE, we would be happy to hear from you.

Contact us at boninabox@geobon.org to discuss your contribution.
